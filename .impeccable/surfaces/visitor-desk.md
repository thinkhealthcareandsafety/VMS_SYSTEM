# Visitor desk surface brief

Mode: operate (guard desk with Visitors / Material / Couriers, host and approver pages, guest pass, admin console). Client: Marriott Marquis New Delhi and St. Regis New Delhi, one shared complex, three gates, one security team. Direction pinned by the client brief (co-branded, black, white, grey, red); no concept roll.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One house livery worn by two hotels. The software is the complex's own instrument, not a generic tool with a logo pasted on: St. Regis supplies the restraint (black, white, silver, a bold sans for the few moments that speak), Marriott Marquis supplies the signature (its red, and the thin red frame it draws around MARQUIS). It refuses the SaaS look: no teal, no tinted cards, no gradients.
OWN-WORLD: Stone-grey ground #F3F3F1, white surfaces, near-black ink #151515 for every interaction (focus, selection, switches, primary buttons). Marriott red #9C1C2E only as signature: the logos, the framed label, selection ticks, chart bars. St. Regis silver #A8A8A8 for the monogram and hairline ornament only, never text. Live and approved stay green, errors keep their own vermilion with icons and words. Geist (600) for page titles and names; Geist for everything operated; tracked capitals only inside the framed label.
STORY: The guard works fast in a calm, expensive-feeling room; the host and the guest see the two hotels they know; the admin sees one complex.
FIRST VIEWPORT (guard): top bar with the two marks (M and SR) divided by a silver hairline, the gate name in a thin red frame in tracked capitals, live state; below it the existing check-in form, titles in bold Geist.
FORM: Operate. Signature: the red-framed label (gate name, pass number frame, login). Brief-pinned, no seed key.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
