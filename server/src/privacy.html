Privacy Policy

AED SmartX Visitor Desk · Last updated 13 October 2026

What this service does

The Visitor Desk is used by security staff at the gate to register visitors and ask the person they are visiting (the host) to approve their entry.

Information we collect

  • Visitors: name, mobile number, gender, optional email, company, purpose of visit, a photo taken at the gate, and an image of their ID with the Aadhaar number masked.
  • Hosts: name, flat or department, mobile number, and optionally email.
  • Entry and exit times, pass numbers and an audit log of approvals.

How it is used

Only to manage entry at the gate: identifying visitors, asking hosts to approve or decline, printing visitor passes, and keeping a record of who was on the premises.

Messages to hosts

Approval requests are sent to the host by WhatsApp, Telegram or SMS. They contain the visitor's name, company, purpose and photo, and a single-use link that expires after a few minutes. Hosts receive messages only on the number registered with the site.

Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data for marketing. Data is processed by the service providers that run the service (hosting, database and messaging providers such as Meta for WhatsApp) only to deliver it.

Retention

Masked ID images are deleted automatically after a short retention period set by the site. Visit records are kept as long as the site needs them for security purposes.

Your choices

You can ask the site administrator to see, correct or delete your information. Hosts can ask to stop receiving requests on WhatsApp at any time.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the AED SmartX site administrator.
